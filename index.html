MICHAEL E. BOWERS

THE MAN BEHIND THE STORIES

A life lived.
A world within.

Veteran. Writer. Observer
of the human experience.

A 30-year veteran of the United States Air Force, Michael draws on a lifetime of travel and personal experience. Originally from Greenville, South Carolina, he is based in Dayton, Ohio.

His work explores the meeting point of lived experience and perception—from psychological fiction to his own account of survival.
